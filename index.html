Who is Jijnāsu?

Jijnāsu publishes English editions of Sanskrit astrological, tantric, and devotional texts that most readers otherwise can't get to — locked behind a scan of a century-old printing, an academic translation too dense to actually read, or simply never rendered into English at all. The goal of each edition is a plain one: the real text, in real English, without losing the tradition's own vocabulary or its depth.

Every translation is checked word by word against traditional dictionaries and grammar, not taken on a first pass. Where the source itself is damaged or genuinely ambiguous, the edition says so plainly rather than papering over it — so what's on the page is either the source's own meaning or a clearly marked editorial judgment, never the two blurred together.
