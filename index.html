Who is Jijnāsu?

Jijnāsu is the name I publish these translations under. A lot of what's worth reading in the Sanskrit astrological, tantric, and devotional traditions is stuck somewhere unreachable — a scan of a hundred-year-old printing, a translation dense enough to need a second translation, or simply never rendered into English at all. I work through these texts myself, checking word choices against traditional dictionaries and grammar rather than taking a first pass at face value, and I'd rather mark a line as uncertain than quietly smooth over something I'm not sure of.

I'm one person doing this carefully, not an institution — which is also why I'd genuinely like to hear from you if something in an edition is wrong. See Errata & Corrections or just write in.
