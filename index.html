Old books, in plain English.

Complete translations of classical Indian texts on tantra, yoga, medicine and astrology. Nothing softened, nothing left out.

See the books↓

The name is Sanskrit for one who wants to know. These books were printed once, in a language most readers cannot follow, and were on their way to being forgotten. We put them into English a curious person can read, in the order they were written, with their mantras in their own words.

How we work

Complete

Every passage of the original, in its own order. If the source says something hard, the English says it too.

Plain

Sentences a reader can follow without a teacher beside them. The tradition's own words are kept and explained once.

Checked

Each passage read against the source page. Each mantra compared letter by letter. Each name looked up.
