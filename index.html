English editions of classical Sanskrit texts

Jijnāsu prepares clean, English-only reading editions of public-domain Sanskrit manuscripts — astrological, tantric, and devotional works that have mostly been available only as scanned originals or dense scholarly translations. Each edition preserves the source's own terminology and structure while removing the scan artifacts, apparatus, and untranslated passages that make the originals hard to actually read.

Where a passage is uncertain, it's marked as such rather than smoothed over; where something has been left out of the reading edition, it's disclosed in a Textual Notes appendix rather than silently dropped.

Rāvaṇa Saṃhitā
Vol. 4

Rāvaṇa Saṃhitā, Volume 4

Traditionally attributed to Rāvaṇa · Translated by Jijnāsu

The fourth chapter of a composite treatise long associated with Rāvaṇa — this volume is a reference work on Kāla (Jyotiṣa) Śāstra, the science of time: the zodiac signs, the twelve houses, the planets and their natures, and an exhaustive catalogue of planetary combinations and their results, argued throughout by citation to named classical authorities.
